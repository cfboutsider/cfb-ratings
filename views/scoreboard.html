Interest is closeness × quality. It ranks games by how close the model expects them to be, weighted by how good both teams are. A pick’em scores highest, and among equally close games the better matchup wins. Filter first, then sort — within a division the quality term flattens and closeness does the work.
Line is the predicted margin from the home team’s point of view; negative means the visitor is favoured. Neutral-site games are marked N and carry no home-field adjustment.
