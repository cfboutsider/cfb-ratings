Two different numbers, and only one is about coaching. Coach effect is the season’s Move, standardised and shrunk by tenure — that is the coaching one, and what this table sorts by. Neither number is purely coaching. Gap is End minus Talent: how far a program rates beyond what its roster predicted. Coach effect is standardised within its season and division and shrunk by how many seasons the coach has on record. A team can carry a large Gap while its coach effect is negative: South Dakota State rated 379 points above its roster in 2025 and still fell 126 points across the year. Both numbers bundle scheme fit, player development, health, continuity and luck. Tenure is also selected: coaches with long tenure are coaches who won, so it will look causal when it is partly survivorship. Read it as “where a coaching story would be if there is one”, not as the story itself.
